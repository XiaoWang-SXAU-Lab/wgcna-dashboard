# ReguTrace plant workflow

1. Read the user's genome FASTA, GFF3, and either CDS or protein FASTA. For CDS, translate coding sequences and use the GFF to resolve local gene IDs. The dashboard accepts an annotation TSV or eggNOG table and uses it for gene names and descriptions.
2. Use PlantTFHunter family rules and HMMER to identify TFs. Keep a complete catalogue including genes with no transferred motif and rows whose family remains ambiguous.
3. Compare identified TF proteins with the JASPAR 2024 plant reference in both directions using BLASTP. First restrict the hit ranking to compatible TF families and collapse local isoforms by gene. Require reciprocal best bit scores, amino-acid identity at least 35%, at least 60% alignment span on each protein, and E-value at most 1e-5. Keep exact score ties. These thresholds are implementation defaults.
4. Transfer the representative matrix of the matched donor and scan the local GFF-defined promoters. The Windows and macOS bundlers use the new plant reference. It contains 615 protein donors with one representative matrix each, chosen by total information content from eligible JASPAR profiles, breaking ties by matrix ID. Record donor accessions and motif IDs in the TF map and derived trace.
5. Report the identified and mapped TF counts, matrices scanned, matrices with a passing site, distinct predicted target genes, and distinct TF–target pairs. The dashboard shows every TF family, a searchable TF catalogue and evidence table, and interactive TF gene structure, protein domain, target gene structure and promoter-site views.

The mapping method follows PlantTFDB's same-family reciprocal best-hit approach. Reference selection and default alignment thresholds are specific to this implementation. See https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5210657/ and https://planttfdb.gao-lab.org/help_bind_motif.php.

HF1 validation used 1,000 bp upstream and a site p-value threshold of 1e-4. CDS supplied through the browser with e.Annotation_gene_transcript.xls produced 1,981 catalogue rows, 1,908 identified, 260 mapped, 257 scanned matrices, 229 matrices with hits, 37,407 distinct target genes, 1,785,471 distinct TF–target pairs, and 3,090,137 evidence rows. PEP CLI had 1,907 identified, with Fta_6G0009940 ambiguous instead of identified. All 260 TF–motif–donor triples matched exactly. In the PEP catalogue, 81 WRKY genes were identified and 17 mapped. The same-family reference remains limited by available curated donor matrices, and the distinct target count is the genome-wide union over all TFs. Site and interaction counts measure computational predictions.
